I'm an Architect in AI Computing at Black Sesame Technologies. My work focuses on AI infrastructure, compute architecture, and automated chip design-space exploration and compute-kernel optimization.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the School of Computing, [National University of Singapore (NUS)](https://www.nus.edu.sg/), working with Prof. [Tulika Mitra](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~tulika/). I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://english.hust.edu.cn/) (2018–2024) under Prof. [Hai Jin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=o02W0aEAAAAJ&hl=en), and a B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from [North China Electric Power University](https://english.ncepu.edu.cn/) (2014–2018).
